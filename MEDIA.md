# Music credits

- **Space Journey**: arrangement of the Star Trek: The Next Generation main title by Jerry Goldsmith, incorporating Alexander Courage's Star Trek fanfare. Source MIDI: [S16.MID](https://www.vic-fontaine.com/countm.php?group=10&nbr=3), listed on the [TNG music page](https://www.vic-fontaine.com/tng.php). The arrangement uses the highest brass voice and low string voice, with the silent introduction removed.
- **Space Journey Fanfare**: arrangement of the Star Trek fanfare by Alexander Courage. Source MIDI: [STARTREK.MID](https://www.vic-fontaine.com/countm.php?group=2&nbr=8), listed on the [original-series music page](https://www.vic-fontaine.com/tos.php). The arrangement contains the first complete eight-note brass call.

The original multitrack MIDI files are not included. These tracks are single-track MIDI files prepared for the controller's 2048-byte per-track limit. The source-code licence does not change rights in the underlying music.
